# TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN: EX-SERIES HYDRAULIC SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING
Document ID: TSB-HYD-2026-04
Machine Scope: Tata Hitachi Hydraulic Excavator ZX210 / EX200
Revision: 1.0
System: High-Pressure Main Hydraulic Circuit

## 1. COMPONENT: HYDRAULIC PUMP & MAIN RELIEF VALVE
- Observed Symptom: Slow boom lifting speed, weak bucket digging breakout force under heavy load, excessive hydraulic oil temperature (>85°C after 30 minutes of continuous operation).
- Primary Inspection:
  1. Measure main pump discharge pressure at test port MP1. Normal operating pressure under full relief load is 280 to 320 bar.
  2. If pressure drops below 190 bar when the oil warms up to 60°C, inspect the pump case drain leakage line.
  3. Case drain flow exceeding 15 L/min at 200 bar indicates severe internal swashplate and piston shoe wear.

## 2. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
- Cause 1: Internal pump leakage caused by axial piston barrel wear and degraded piston slipper retainers.
- Cause 2: Main relief valve spring fatigue or contamination in the pilot orifice preventing the valve from holding 280 bar nominal pressure.

## 3. RECOMMENDED REMEDIAL ACTION
- Action A: Remove main relief valve cartridge, clean pilot damping orifice with solvent, inspect poppet seat for cavitation pitting, and re-torque to 65 Nm.
- Action B: If internal leakage persists, replace the main hydraulic pump assembly (Part No. TH-9204481) and replace high-pressure filter cartridge. Flush hydraulic reservoir and refill with fresh ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil.
- Verification Criteria: Ensure system pressure holds steadily at 280 bar at 65°C oil temperature with cycle time under 3.5 seconds.
